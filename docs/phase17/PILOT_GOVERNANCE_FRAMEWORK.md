## 2. Key Roles & Mandates

### 2.1 Pilot Steering Committee (PSC)
- **Role:** Overarching executive authority overseeing pilot milestones, resource allocation, and regulatory compliance.
- **Composition:** Senior representative from DoH Abu Dhabi, ADPHC Population Health Director, and Lead Health System Architect.
- **Decision Authority:** Authorizes progression between pilot stages and approves protocol modifications.

### 2.2 Clinical Safety Officer (CSO)
- **Role:** Independent licensed physician responsible for clinical hazard monitoring and safety incident evaluation (DCB0129 / ISO 14971).
- **Veto Power:** CSO holds unilateral authority to pause or halt the pilot immediately if unmitigated patient safety risks emerge.

### 2.3 Data Protection Officer (DPO) & Ethics Board
- **Role:** Enforces UAE Health Data Law (Fed Law No. 2/2019) and ADHICS v2 compliance.
- **Mandate:** Manages Institutional Review Board (IRB) ethical submissions, ensures informed consent protocols, and audits data access logs.

### 2.4 Clinical Site Leads (SEHA Primary Healthcare Centers)
- **Role:** Supervises participating primary care physicians, nursing staff, and Community Health Workers across designated pilot clinics in Abu Dhabi Central, Al Ain, and Al Dhafra.
