## 2. Health Economic Return on Investment (ROI)
Preventive precision screening generates substantial cost avoidance for health payers (Daman / Thiqa) and health authorities:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   ANNUAL COST AVOIDANCE ESTIMATES                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Early Colorectal Cancer Detection (Stage I vs Stage IV): 18.5M AED   │
│ • Prevention of Acute Myocardial Infarction / Stroke:       24.2M AED   │
│ • Avoidance of End-Stage Renal Disease via Early IFHAS:    14.0M AED   │
│ • Total Annual System Cost Avoidance at Scale:             56.7M AED   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Projected Benefit-Cost Ratio:** **24.6 : 1** (For every 1 AED invested in operational technology, 24.6 AED in catastrophic acute treatment costs is avoided over a 5-year cohort horizon).
- **Sustainability Model:** Funded via public health prevention allocation and value-based healthcare shared-savings agreements with national health insurers.
