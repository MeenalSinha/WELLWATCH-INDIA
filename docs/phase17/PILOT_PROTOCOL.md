# WELLWATCH PREVENT — Clinical Pilot Study Protocol
**Study Title:** Prospective Multi-Center Clinical Validation of Life-Course Preventive Care Intelligence in Abu Dhabi  
**Protocol ID:** WWP-PILOT-2026-V1  
**Study Duration:** 6 Months  
**Target Enrollment:** 4,500 Participants (3,000 Adults, 1,500 Pediatrics)

---

## 1. Study Sites & Geography
1. **Site 1 (Metropolitan Urban):** Al Bateen Healthcare Center (Abu Dhabi Central) — Primary adult population and chronic screening cohort.
2. **Site 2 (Family & Multi-Generational):** Al Hili Healthcare Center (Al Ain) — High-density family health and pediatric developmental cohort.
3. **Site 3 (Remote / Rural Outreach):** Madinat Zayed Healthcare Center & Mobile Health Unit (Al Dhafra) — Blue-collar workforce and rural outreach.

---

## 2. Participant Eligibility Criteria

### 2.1 Adult Cohort (IFHAS Prevention & Screening)
- **Inclusion Criteria:**
  - Age $\ge 18$ years.
  - Resident of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi (valid Emirates ID).
  - Eligible for ADPHC IFHAS comprehensive screening or routine preventive checkup.
  - Ability to provide informed consent (or authorized legal representative).
- **Exclusion Criteria:**
  - Acute unstable medical emergency requiring immediate hospitalization.
  - Terminal illness with life expectancy $< 6$ months.
  - Inability or refusal to participate in digital follow-up.

### 2.2 Pediatric Cohort (Well-Child Development)
- **Inclusion Criteria:**
  - Age $0$ to $18$ years.
  - Presenting for scheduled well-child visit or routine developmental assessment.
  - Valid parental / guardian informed consent per Wadeema's Law.
- **Exclusion Criteria:**
  - Known complex genetic syndromic condition under active tertiary specialty care.

---

## 3. Primary & Secondary Endpoints
- **Primary Endpoint:** Proportion of overdue preventive care gaps successfully closed with verified clinical evidence within 90 days.
- **Secondary Endpoints:**
  1. Clinician agreement rate with generated preventive recommendations (target $> 92\%$).
  2. Patient screening appointment attendance rate (target $> 75\%$).
  3. Time-to-action for critical cardiometabolic alerts (target $< 14$ days).
  4. System Usability Scale (SUS) score among clinicians and Community Health Workers (target $> 80$).

---

## 4. Safety Stop Criteria
The Clinical Safety Officer will pause or terminate the pilot if:
1. Any adverse clinical event occurs directly resulting from software erroneous guidance.
2. Recommendation discordance with senior clinician review exceeds $10\%$.
3. Data breach or unauthorized cross-tenant data exposure occurs.
