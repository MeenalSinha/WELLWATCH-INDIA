# WELLWATCH PREVENT — Production Rollout Strategy (Stages 1 to 6)
**Target Market:** Universal Population Health Coverage in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates  
**Governing Standard:** ADPHC Digital Health Transformation Strategy 2026–2030  
**Rollout Framework:** 6-Stage Governed Expansion

---

---

## 2. Stage-by-Stage Implementation Plan

### Stage 1: Shadow Deployment & Sandbox Verification (Month 0)
- **Scope:** Zero live patient communication; parallel chart ingestion of historical encounters.
- **Goal:** Validate FHIR parser throughput and algorithm calibration in sovereign cloud infrastructure.
- **Go-Gate:** 100% agreement on 500 retrospective benchmark encounters.

### Stage 2: Controlled Clinical Pilot (Months 1–6)
- **Scope:** 3 primary healthcare centers (Al Bateen, Al Hili, Madinat Zayed); 4,500 enrolled participants.
- **Goal:** Validate care-gap closure acceleration, CHW offline synchronization, and clinician usability.
- **Go-Gate:** Overall SUS $> 80$, Care-gap closure increase $> 25\%$, zero critical clinical software events.

### Stage 3: DoH Steering Committee Review & Production Approval (Month 7)
- **Scope:** Formal review by Department of Health (DoH) Abu Dhabi and ADPHC leadership.
- **Goal:** Regulatory sign-off, clinical safety verification, and authorization for Wave 1 expansion.

### Stage 4: Wave 1 Expansion — 15 Primary Care Centers (Months 8–12)
- **Scope:** Expansion across SEHA Ambulatory Healthcare Services in Abu Dhabi metropolitan area and Al Ain.
- **Target Reach:** 50,000 active annual preventive screenings.

### Stage 5: Western Region & Universal CHW Deployment (Months 13–18)
- **Scope:** Full coverage across Al Dhafra desert communities, industrial worker residential cities, and remote settlements via connected mobile clinics.
- **Target Reach:** Closing geographic disparity gap to $< 3\%$ across all regions.

### Stage 6: Universal Emirate-Wide Coverage (Months 19–24)
- **Scope:** Integration across all SEHA public clinics and participating private healthcare networks (M42, Mediclinic, VPS).
- **Target Reach:** Universal life-course preventive care intelligence for 3.0M residents.
