# WELLWATCH PREVENT
## Child Well-Child Pathway Specification (Phase 6)

**Document ID:** SPEC-PH6-01  
**Target Market:** Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates  
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED & VERIFIED  

---

## 1. Guideline Attribution & Legal Basis
- **Publishing Authority:** Department of Health Abu Dhabi (DoH) & Abu Dhabi Public Health Centre (ADPHC).
- **Clinical Standard:** Abu Dhabi Well-Child Clinical Guidelines (0 to 6 Years).
- **Statutory Protection:** UAE Federal Law No. 3 of 2016 on Child Rights (Wadeema's Law), guaranteeing comprehensive preventive care for all children in the UAE.

## 2. Schedule of Milestones (0 to 72 Months)

| Milestone ID | Visit Name | Target Age | Target Window | Core Screening Components |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| `WC-00M` | Newborn Hospital Assessment | Birth | 0–1 months | Metabolic panel, hearing screen, CCHD, physical exam. |
| `WC-02M` | 2-Month Well-Child Visit | 2 months | 1–3 months | Growth percentiles, motor/sensory check, immunization reconciliation. |
| `WC-04M` | 4-Month Well-Child Visit | 4 months | 3–5 months | Growth, social/emotional milestones, physical exam. |
| `WC-06M` | 6-Month Well-Child Visit | 6 months | 5–8 months | Nutrition assessment, developmental screening, oral health guidance. |
| `WC-09M` | 9-Month Well-Child Visit | 9 months | 8–11 months | Standardized developmental screening (ASQ-3/CDC), anemia check. |
| `WC-12M` | 12-Month Milestone | 12 months | 11–15 months | Hemoglobin anemia screen, dental check, motor milestones. |
| `WC-18M` | 18-Month Well-Child Visit | 18 months | 16–21 months | Autism Spectrum Disorder screening (M-CHAT-R/F), speech evaluation. |
| `WC-24M` | 24-Month Well-Child Visit | 24 months | 22–30 months | Autism follow-up, BMI screening, behavioral development. |
| `WC-36M` | 36-Month (3 Years) Visit | 36 months | 31–42 months | Vision screening, hearing screen, blood pressure baseline. |
| `WC-48M` | 48-Month (4 Years) Visit | 48 months | 43–54 months | School readiness, vision acuity, hearing, oral health. |
| `WC-60M` | 60-Month (5 Years) Visit | 60 months | 55–66 months | Physical exam, immunization booster check, visual acuity. |
| `WC-72M` | 72-Month (6 Years) Visit | 72 months | 67–78 months | School health transition check, growth, vitals baseline. |
