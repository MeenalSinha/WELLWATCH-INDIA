# WELLWATCH PREVENT
## Adult IFHAS Pathway Specification (Phase 6)

**Document ID:** SPEC-PH6-02  
**Target Market:** Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates  
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED & VERIFIED  

---

## 1. Guideline Attribution & Legal Basis
- **Publishing Authority:** Abu Dhabi Public Health Centre (ADPHC) & Department of Health Abu Dhabi (DoH).
- **Clinical Standard:** IFHAS Comprehensive Periodic Health Examination Programme.
- **Coverage Cohort:** All adults (18+ years) residing in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi.
- **Recurrence Interval:** Every 36 months (3 years) for comprehensive baseline; annual for dental and high-risk recalls.

| Module ID | Module Name | Target Cohort | Recurrence | Key Clinical Diagnostics |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| `IFHAS-CARDIOVASCULAR` | Cardiovascular & Hypertension | Age 18+ | Every 3 years | Blood pressure, heart rate, BMI, ASCVD 10-year risk estimation. |
